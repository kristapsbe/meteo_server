In short: Solitaire is a free, ad-free solitaire game that works offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share user data.

1. Overview

Solitaire is a free, ad-free solitaire game designed to work entirely offline. We respect your privacy. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or other user data.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information or user data.

The app does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name or email address
  • Phone numbers or contacts
  • Location information
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking identifiers
  • IP addresses for collection or tracking purposes
  • Photos, videos, or other files
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Browsing or search history
  • Usage analytics
  • Advertising or marketing information
  • Account or authentication information
  • Payment information

3. Internet and Network Access

Solitaire is designed to function without an internet connection. The app does not connect to our servers or any other remote service to collect or transmit user information.

4. Advertising

Solitaire is ad-free. We do not display advertisements and do not use advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party services that collect or transmit user data. This includes advertising, analytics, and other online data-collection services.

6. Data Storage

The app does not maintain a remote account or cloud database containing information about you.

Any game-related information that may be stored locally on your device, such as game settings or progress, remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

7. Data Sharing and Sale

Because we do not collect user data:

  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not share user data with third parties.
  • We do not provide user data to advertisers.
  • We do not use user data for targeted advertising.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not collect or maintain personal data on our servers. Therefore, there is no personal information held by us that needs to be deleted.

If the app stores game-related information locally on your device, you can generally remove that information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to the behavior of your Android device and operating system.

9. Children's Privacy

Solitaire does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, its privacy practices are the same regardless of the user's age.

10. Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain a server-side database of user information. The app is designed to operate offline and without transmitting user data over the internet.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted at the location where this Privacy Policy is published, with a revised Effective Date.

If a future version of the app introduces data collection, advertising, analytics, online services, or other functionality that affects user privacy, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices.
